Keep or Cut

Support and frequently asked questions.

Keep or Cut helps you delete photos you do not need. It shows photos from your library in random order, thirty at a time, so each one has to justify itself without the surrounding series talking you out of deleting it.

How to use it

  1. Grant access to your photo library when asked.
  2. Tap Start a round.
  3. Swipe left, or tap the trash button, to mark a photo for deletion. Swipe right, or tap the heart, to keep it. Undo reverses the last swipe.
  4. At the end of the round you see a grid of everything marked for deletion. Tap any photo to rescue it.
  5. Confirm, and iOS asks you once to approve the deletion.

Questions

I deleted a photo by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and stay there for 30 days. Open Photos, go to Albums, scroll to Recently Deleted, and recover it.

Why does it need full photo library access?

Limited access only exposes photos you pick by hand, which defeats the point — the app draws at random from your whole library, and it cannot delete a photo it cannot see.

Why are the photos in random order?

Because chronological order is why culling fails. Several shots of one moment rebuild the context of that moment, nostalgia takes over, and nothing gets deleted. Random order breaks that.

Can I see a photo again after keeping it?

Keeping retires a photo from the pool permanently, which is what lets the app converge — every round is thirty photos closer to done. If you want to start over, Reset judgments in Settings makes every photo still in your library eligible again. It cannot bring back photos you deleted.

What happens to favourites?

Photos marked as favourites are never shown. They are already declared keepers.

Does the app upload anything?

No. Keep or Cut has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, and contains no networking code. See the privacy policy.

Are videos included?

Not yet. The current version works on photos only.
