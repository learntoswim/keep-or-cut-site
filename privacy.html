Privacy Policy

Keep or Cut · Effective 21 August 2026

Keep or Cut does not collect any data. It has no servers, no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere. The app contains no networking code at all.

What the app accesses

Keep or Cut asks for access to your photo library. It needs read access to show you your photos and write access to delete the ones you choose to delete. That permission is used for nothing else.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored in two files inside its own container on your device. Neither file ever leaves your device.

  • judgments.json — the identifiers of photos you decided to keep, so the app does not show you the same photo twice, and a running count of how many photos have been deleted.
  • ledger.json — anonymous totals used for the app's statistics: how many photos were kept or deleted per year, month, hour of day and photo type, total pixel counts, and approximate places rounded to roughly 11 km. These are counts only. No photo, thumbnail, filename, or precise location is stored.

Deleting photos

Deletion is performed by iOS, not by Keep or Cut. Every deletion is confirmed by the standard system dialog, and deleted photos are moved to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they remain recoverable for 30 days.

Removing your data

Deleting the app from your device removes both files and everything in them. You can also use Reset judgments in the app's settings to clear the record of which photos you have already decided about. Neither action can restore photos that were deleted — those are handled by the Photos app.

Children

Keep or Cut is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
